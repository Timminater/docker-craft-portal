# Official Craft app icons

The unmodified PNG icons are from `assets/app-icons/` in the official
[Craft Launcher repository](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher), at the
commit in SOURCES.json. Copyright (c) 2026 ArtCraft Team and each app's
contributors. Used under the included MIT license (upstream also offers Apache-2.0).
These identify the individual apps; this portal retains its own logo and branding.

They are bundled offline fallbacks. The portal caches the exact official site's
`/images/apps/<slug>/icon.webp` and `hero.webp` when update networking is enabled,
and serves them locally. No visitor request is sent to the official site.
